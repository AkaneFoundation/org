Privacy Policy

Except where you voluntarily contact the Service Provider (for example by sending a crash report or email), this application does not collect, store or process personal data. No data is transmitted to the Service Provider by the application. Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

If you use the "Crash log" feature, the app opens your device's default email application with a prefilled email template and human-readable text file attachment. You can review and edit the message before deciding whether to send it. We receive only the information you choose to include in the email you send, including attachments. The information included in these emails is only used to fix the crash. For this, the User Provided Data may be anonymized through removal of all personal information, and anonymized information could then be shared with third parties, such as Google, when reporting a bug in Google Issue Tracker. This sharing is done only if deemed required and appropriate to investigate the issue by the Service Provider - if possible, the Service Provider will use alternative ways to investigate the issue that do not require sharing of any information at all. User Provided Data will not and will never be sold to any other third parties. The personal data collected to fix the crash - namely, the email address from which the crash report was received - will be deleted by the Service Provider once the issue is considered solved, but anonymized data may remain indefinitely in bug tracking systems for historical reference. If you do not consent to this, do not send "Crash log" emails in your device's email program to the Service Provider. To revoke consent, contact the Service Provider and all data associated with the contacting email address will be removed by the Service Provider.

If you contact us per E-Mail, we may keep communication records for processing your request and as context for any future communication. These will not be shared by the Service Provider with any third party. If you wish for your communication records to be deleted, contact us.
